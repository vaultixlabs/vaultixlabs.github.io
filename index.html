How it works

Three checks at the door.
One decision you can defend.

01

Guided capture

Edge analysis in the browser coaches the user in real time — lighting, position, glare — before anything reaches your servers.

02

Liveness & match

Certified presentation-attack detection (iBeta PAD Level 2) plus a 1:1 face match against the document. The evidence is signed at birth.

03

Risk decision

A tri-vector score — biometrics, liveness, device reputation — returns approve, step-up, or block, with a cryptographic audit trail.

Capture experience

Errors that guide, not reject.

Legacy vendors answer a bad photo with a generic "document invalid". Vaultix Gate inspects the frame locally and tells the user exactly what to fix — conversationally, accessibly, in their language.

  • Real-time luminance, alignment and glare analysis on-device
  • Conversational instructions instead of dead-end error codes
  • Every correction announced to screen readers via ARIA live regions

Data custody

Biometrics you can't leak.

Face embeddings are born encrypted under keys held by your institution — never under ours. Revoke the key and every biometric template becomes mathematically unrecoverable, for us included.

  • Client-held KMS keys per tenant — true crypto-shredding
  • Vectors stored separately from PII metadata, always
  • EU cells keep European biometric data in Europe
