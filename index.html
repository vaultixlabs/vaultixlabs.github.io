How it works

Four checks at the door.
One decision you can defend.

Every verification runs the same pipeline: read the document, prove the person is real, match the photo, score the risk and hand your team a decision with cryptographic evidence attached.

01

Document OCR

OCR reads the ID document, pulls out the key data and flags anything that looks altered or inconsistent.

02

Guided capture

The browser coaches the user in real time on lighting, position and glare before anything is sent.

03

Liveness and photo match

A certified liveness test proves the person is really there, then their photo is matched with the one on the document.

04

Risk decision

A risk score returns approve, step up or block. Your team can review, override and export every decision with its evidence.

Capture experience

Errors that guide, not reject.

Most verification tools answer a bad photo with a generic "document invalid" and the user gives up. Vaultix Gate checks the image right on the user's device and tells them exactly what to fix, in plain language and in their own language.

  • Checks brightness, framing and glare in real time, before the photo is even sent
  • Plain-language guidance instead of dead-end error codes
  • Every correction is announced to screen readers through Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) live regions

Data custody

Your Biometrics. Your Keys. Your Control.

Biometric templates are encrypted from creation using dedicated keys controlled by your organization, never Vaultix. Revoke access, and those templates become inaccessible, even to us.

  • Your keys, your control: dedicated AWS Key Management Service (KMS) encryption keys for every client, revocable at any time
  • Data separation: biometric templates are stored separately from personal information
  • Regional data residency: sensitive data stays within the regional infrastructure serving your organization; for EU customers, biometric data is stored in Europe to support General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance

Security and privacy

Security you can demonstrate. Compliance you can substantiate.

Vaultix Gate's security architecture is documented end-to-end: how data flows, who controls encryption keys, how long information is retained, and how each control supports compliance requirements. Built to give internal compliance teams, regulators, external auditors, and other interested parties the transparency and evidence they need. No black boxes.

Nothing biometric stored in the clear

Face images and biometric data are never written to disk or logs unencrypted. Raw captures are deleted after processing; encrypted templates and a limited set of audit frames are retained as evidence.

Keys under your organization's control

Each client has dedicated encryption keys and retains control of the key material. Revoking a key makes the protected templates unrecoverable, even for Vaultix Labs.

Regional data residency

Data is stored in regional infrastructure across the United States, Latin America, and Europe. For European clients, biometric data remains within Europe, supporting GDPR compliance.

Cryptographically signed audit trail

An append-only event log, with legally recognized timestamps (RFC 3161) and ECDSA digital signatures on every decision and evidence export. Verifiable records for audits, inspections, and internal reviews.

Consent before capture

Explicit, localized consent is recorded before biometric capture begins and added to the audit log. No consent, no capture.

Accessible by design

WCAG 2.2 AA throughout: screen-reader guidance, full keyboard navigation, and optional audio cues.

FAQ

Questions compliance teams ask us first.

Where is our users' biometric data stored?

Vaultix Gate operates across the United States, Latin America, and Europe. For customers in the European Union, biometric data is stored in Europe, supporting GDPR compliance.

Can Vaultix Labs access our users' face templates?

No. Face data is encrypted with keys controlled by your organization. Vaultix operates the matching infrastructure but never holds the key material, making templates unreadable to us.

What happens to our data if we terminate the service?

Revoking or destroying your encryption keys makes every stored biometric template mathematically unrecoverable, even to Vaultix. Metadata is deleted or exported according to your retention policy.

How does Vaultix Gate support PSD2 and GDPR compliance?

The platform supports GDPR data minimization and EU data residency. Approved verifications can issue a signed SCA attestation for the Payment Services Directive 2 (PSD2) "something you are" factor. We also provide DPIA documentation, including data-flow maps and records of processing.

What can our compliance team see?

A dedicated dashboard provides the full verification queue, risk scores based on three signals (face match, liveness, and device reputation), configurable signal weighting, and a review queue for borderline cases. Exportable evidence packages include timestamps and digital signatures.

How long is verification data retained?

Retention is configurable per client. Audit frames are stored in tamper-resistant storage, with a default retention period of 12 to 24 months to support dispute resolution and regulatory evidence requirements. Metadata follows your defined retention policy.

Is the verification flow accessible?

Yes. The capture widget meets WCAG 2.2 AA, with full keyboard navigation, screen-reader guidance, optional audio cues, and conversational error messages in your users' language.

How can we integrate Vaultix Gate?

Choose the integration path that fits your architecture: embed our capture web component in your application, use our hosted verification page, or build a custom experience through direct API integration. Your backend can receive signed webhooks for verification events, and you can test the integration in a sandbox first.
