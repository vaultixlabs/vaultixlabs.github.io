Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Draft — pending legal review

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between [Customer legal entity] ("Controller") and Vaultix Labs ([legal entity name], "Processor") governing use of the Vaultix Gate service. It reflects the requirements of GDPR Article 28 and equivalent data-protection laws.

1. Roles and scope

The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing end users' personal data. The Processor processes personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, as described in the Service documentation and API contract.

2. Subject matter, duration and nature of processing

ElementDetail
Subject matterBiometric and device-based identity verification for user onboarding
DurationFor the term of the Service agreement plus the retention period defined by the Controller
NatureCollection via capture widget/hosted page, biometric matching, risk scoring, evidence storage, audit logging, webhook delivery
PurposeIdentity verification, fraud checks, regulatory compliance and audit evidence

3. Categories of personal data and data subjects

  • Data subjects: individuals onboarded by the Controller (end users).
  • Data categories: identity metadata (user reference, document type/country), facial images and biometric embeddings, device and network signals, verification events, consent records.
  • Special categories: biometric data (GDPR Art. 9) — processed solely on the basis of explicit consent captured in the verification flow.

4. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions, including for international transfers, unless required by law;
  • Ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • Implement the technical and organizational measures described in Annex A;
  • Engage subprocessors only with the Controller's general authorization, under written terms imposing equivalent data-protection obligations; maintain and share the current subprocessor list;
  • Assist the Controller, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to data-subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection);
  • Assist with DPIA/EIPD and prior-consultation obligations;
  • At the end of services, delete or return all personal data at the Controller's choice, unless retention is required by law; biometric templates are rendered unrecoverable upon key revocation;
  • Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow audits as described in Section 7.

5. Security measures (summary — Annex A)

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (dedicated per-tenant keys, revocable by the customer, with envelope encryption);
  • Zero-persistence handling of plaintext biometrics; ciphertext-only template storage;
  • Regional data residency — EU cell in AWS eu-west-1;
  • Append-only, hash-chained audit log with write-once storage, RFC 3161 timestamps and ECDSA signatures;
  • Explicit consent gate before any biometric capture; consent hash appended to audit trail;
  • Separation of biometric vectors from personal metadata; blind indexing;
  • Access controls, least-privilege IAM, no biometric data in logs.

6. Subprocessors and international transfers

Current subprocessors are limited to Amazon Web Services entities in the regions hosting the relevant cell. Where data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, the Processor ensures appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent). The Controller may object to new subprocessors on reasonable data-protection grounds.

7. Audits

Upon reasonable request, the Processor will provide information and, where justified by the risk, allow audits or inspections subject to confidentiality and reasonable scheduling. Audits are at the Controller's cost unless they reveal material non-compliance.

8. Data subject rights and breach notification

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach, providing information reasonably necessary for the Controller to meet its notification obligations. The Processor assists with data-subject requests as described in Section 4.

9. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the main agreement, except where mandatory law provides otherwise.

10. Term

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the Service agreement and the period necessary to return or delete personal data.
