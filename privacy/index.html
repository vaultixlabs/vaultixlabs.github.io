Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Draft — pending legal review

Vaultix Labs ([legal entity name], "Vaultix", "we", "us") operates the Vaultix Gate identity verification platform and this website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process, why, where it is stored, and the rights you have. It applies both to visitors of this website and, in the manner described below, to the end users whose identity is verified through Vaultix Gate on behalf of our customers.

1. Roles: controller vs. processor

Website visitors. For data collected through this website (for example, messages you send us), Vaultix Labs acts as the data controller.

Vaultix Gate customers' end users. Our customers — banks, neobanks, fintechs and other regulated institutions — decide why and how personal data of their users is processed. They are the data controllers. Vaultix Labs acts as a data processor under GDPR Article 28 and equivalent laws, processing data only on the customer's documented instructions.

2. Data we process

2.1 Through this website

This website is a static site. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The contact form does not transmit data to our servers — it opens your email client with a pre-filled message, so nothing you type is stored by us. If you email us, we process your name, email address and message content solely to respond to your inquiry.

2.2 Through the Vaultix Gate service

When a customer integrates Vaultix Gate, we process on their behalf:

  • Account/identity metadata provided by the customer (user reference, document type, document country).
  • Biometric data: face images and face embeddings used for liveness detection and 1:1 matching. Biometric templates are special-category data under GDPR Article 9 and are processed only with the explicit consent recorded before capture.
  • Device and network signals used for risk scoring (device reputation, proxy/VPN indicators).
  • Verification events: scores, decisions, timestamps and evidence records.

Vaultix never receives or stores payment card data, government ID numbers in clear text beyond what the customer sends, or credentials to the customer's systems.

3. Legal bases and purposes

  • Performance of contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): delivering verification results to the customer.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)): biometric processing, captured and recorded inside the verification flow before any biometric capture occurs.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): records needed for regulatory audit and dispute evidence.

4. Where data is stored

The Vaultix Gate EU cell runs entirely on AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland). European personal data is stored and processed in the EU. Regional cells are designed so that each market's data remains in its own jurisdiction. Biometric templates are stored separately from personal metadata and are encrypted at rest under per-tenant keys controlled by the customer.

5. Retention

Retention is configured per customer. Evidence frames used for audit and dispute purposes default to 12–24 months in write-once storage; other data follows the retention policy agreed with each customer. Upon termination or key revocation, biometric templates become cryptographically unrecoverable.

6. Subprocessors and transfers

We rely on Amazon Web Services (AWS) as our infrastructure subprocessors. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, we use appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms). The current subprocessor list is available to customers under the Data Processing Agreement.

7. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures including encryption in transit and at rest, per-tenant key isolation, zero-persistence handling of plaintext biometrics, append-only signed audit trails, and regional data residency. Details are documented in our security architecture and available to customers for review.

8. Your rights

If you are an end user of a Vaultix Gate customer, please direct access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection requests to that customer — they are the controller. Vaultix assists them in fulfilling these requests. Website visitors may contact us at hello@vaultixlabs.com to exercise their rights or lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

9. Children

Vaultix Gate is a B2B service not directed at children. Our customers are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to verify the individuals they onboard.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced on this page. Questions or requests: hello@vaultixlabs.com · [postal address].
